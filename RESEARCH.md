# Research use

PANINI v1.0 is an open research compiler platform.

Use it to:

- compile explicit sequence programs to Sequence IR + ATGC
- represent unresolved biological intent as diagnostics and work orders
- plug in organism models without editing Core
- execute reduced-order digital twins with inspectable traces
- keep desired, predicted, observed, and validated artifacts separate

Do not cite a fixture run as experimental validation.
Do not cite DESIGN_UNRESOLVED as a failed compiler.
