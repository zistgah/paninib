# PANINI v0.7 — Biological Resolution

v0.6 implemented the deterministic sequence compiler and the honest
`DESIGN_UNRESOLVED` boundary. v0.7 adds the resolution spine. It does
not invent genomes.

## What v0.6 already implemented

Explicit sequence compilation through CISC/RISC/Sequence IR to ATGC;
living knowledge record schema; two-path architecture; two-tail mouse
simulates and remains genotype-unresolved.

## What v0.7 adds

Per-constraint BiologicalResolutionEngine; diagnostics E0001–E0011;
schema-first ExperimentalIR and ResolutionWorkOrder; partial compilation;
registry/standard-library path; USER_ASSERTED causality; generative
interface (unavailable by default); multidimensional coverage; knowledge-growth invariant.

## What remains unresolved

Causal genotype for a two-tail mouse; inverse LoF mappings; ab-initio
sequences presented as established biology; experimental validation of
compiled ATGC; encyclopedic organism databases.

## Knowledge-Closure Invariant

Any previously unresolved constraint whose missing information is
representable by PANINI knowledge/provider/registry/evidence/model
contracts MUST become resolvable by adding those records without
modifying compiler source.

## ResolutionWorkOrder

Names the biological question, missing relation, evidence class, knowledge
record to create, and compiler artifacts unblocked. Not a wet-lab protocol.
Integration-neutral.
