## Scientific boundary

Explicit / evidence-backed sequence transformations compile to ATGC.

A phenotype with no validated causal sequence mapping returns
`DESIGN_UNRESOLVED` and does not invent nucleotides.

The two-tail mouse program still simulates. Its genotype realization
is unresolved.
